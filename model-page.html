Frequently asked

FAQs about the Curvv EV.

What is the real-world range of the Tata Curvv EV?
Tata claims 502 km on the 55 kWh pack (MIDC test cycle) and 330 km on the 45 kWh pack. Owner-reported real-world range is 380-420 km on the 55 kWh in mixed city + highway driving, and 250-280 km on the 45 kWh. AC use, expressway speeds above 100 km/h, and full-load conditions reduce range by another 8-12%. The 55 kWh is the practical pick for anyone doing regular outstation drives.
How long does the Curvv EV take to charge?
On a 70 kW DC fast charger (Empowered+ variants), 10-80% takes about 40 minutes. The 50 kW DC option (lower variants) takes around 56 minutes for the same. AC home charging (3.3 kW): 16 hours full. AC home charging (7.2 kW wallbox): 8 hours full. Tata Power has 5,500+ public chargers across India, with many concentrated on key highway corridors.
Which Curvv EV variant should I buy?
For most buyers: Accomplished+ 55 (₹19.99 L ex-showroom) hits the sweet spot — full 502 km range, 70 kW DC charging, panoramic sunroof, and JBL audio. If you need ADAS Level 2, V2L, ventilated seats, and 360° camera, jump to Empowered+ 55 (₹20.99 L). Skip the 45 kWh pack unless you're a strict urban user with home charging — the 330 km range can feel limiting.
What is the warranty on the battery?
The high-voltage battery comes with an 8-year / 1,60,000 km warranty (whichever comes first). The standard vehicle warranty is 3 years / unlimited kilometers, extendable up to 5 years. Tata also offers comprehensive battery roadside coverage and a guaranteed minimum 70% battery capacity at the warranty endpoint.
Does the Curvv EV qualify for any government EV subsidies?
Yes — being an electric vehicle, it qualifies for zero road tax in most states (saves ₹1.7-2.5 lakh in RTO charges). State-level subsidies vary: Delhi offers ₹1.5 lakh on EVs under ₹15 lakh ex-showroom, Maharashtra offers ₹15,000-25,000, Gujarat similar. The Curvv EV is locally manufactured, so it qualifies for FAME-2 incentives that have been factored into the ex-showroom price.
Is V2L (Vehicle to Load) useful in real life?
V2L lets you use the car's battery to power external 230V appliances (up to 3.3 kW). Owners report using it for: camping trips, outdoor events, power tools, occasional home backup during outages. The 55 kWh battery can run a 1.5-ton AC for ~20 hours or a household fridge + lights for 2-3 days in emergency. It's available on Empowered+ variants only.
How does the Curvv EV compare to MG ZS EV and BYD Atto 3?
The Curvv EV undercuts MG ZS EV (₹18.99-25.21 L) and BYD Atto 3 (₹24.99-33.99 L) on price while offering competitive range. Curvv EV's advantages: ADAS L2 standard on top variants, panoramic sunroof, V2L, lower service costs, wider Tata service network. BYD has better brand cachet and slightly more refined cabin; MG offers better warranty and pricing on entry variants. Curvv EV is the best value-for-money pick in this segment.

Should you wait for the October refresh?

Wait if you...

  • Drive 100+ km daily and need maximum range
  • Want AWD for hill stations, rough roads, or all-weather grip
  • Want ADAS Level 2 but can't stretch to the Empowered+ trim today
  • Are flexible on timeline and don't need delivery in the next 3-4 months
  • Prefer the limited Stealth Edition styling

Buy now if you...

  • Drive under 80 km daily — current 502 km range is more than enough
  • Don't need AWD (most urban buyers don't)
  • Want today's pricing — facelift is expected to add ₹1-1.5 L premium
  • Need delivery before September 2026
  • Want to take advantage of current launch offers and exchange bonuses

Our take: If you're a daily commuter under 80 km and don't need AWD, buy now and save ₹1-1.5 L. If range, AWD, or full ADAS matters and you can wait 5-6 months, the October refresh is worth the patience.
