On-road price FAQs.

Common questions about how on-road price works in India, what's included, and how to verify dealer quotes.

What is the difference between ex-showroom and on-road price?+
Ex-showroom is the manufacturer's listed price set uniformly across India. On-road price is what you actually pay after adding state RTO road tax (varies 5–18% by state), 1st-year comprehensive insurance (~3–4% of ex-showroom), TCS @1% for bikes over ₹10 lakh, dealer handling, registration, and Fastag. For most bikes, on-road is 10–18% higher than ex-showroom; for EVs in states with 100% road tax waiver, the gap is much smaller (around 7–8%).
Why is the Hunter 350 on-road price different in each city?+
RTO road tax is set by each state, not the central government, ranging from 6% to 12% for two-wheelers. Insurance also varies by state because IRDAI calculates third-party premiums using regional accident data. Dealer handling charges differ slightly too. For the Hunter 350 in 2026, on-road price ranges from ₹1.94 L (Ahmedabad) to ₹2.03 L (Bangalore) for Metro Rebel.
Is the FAME-II subsidy already included in ex-showroom?+
Yes. The central FAME-II subsidy (₹10,000 per kWh, capped at 15% of ex-showroom for bikes under ₹15 L) is automatically deducted by the manufacturer from the published ex-showroom price. The Hunter 350 is priced post-subsidy. Some states offer additional subsidies on top (Delhi, Maharashtra, Telangana) — these are usually credited at registration, not at booking.
Is TCS (1%) refundable?+
Yes, fully. TCS (Tax Collected at Source under section 206C(1F)) of 1% on bikes over ₹10 lakh is collected by the dealer and deposited with the income tax department in your PAN. You claim it back when filing your Income Tax Return — it appears in your Form 26AS. For the Hunter 350 at ₹1.74 L, the TCS is around ₹19,990 — refundable.
Can dealers charge more than the on-road price shown?+
Dealers can add reasonable charges for accessories, extended warranty, ceramic coating, and similar optional services — but not above the manufacturer's ex-showroom price for the bike itself. RTO and insurance are standardized; if quoted significantly higher, ask for the breakdown. Some dealers add "logistics," "PDI," or "customer benefit" line items — these are usually negotiable, especially during festive months.
