Background

About Rohan

Rohan Saxena joined Autoyugg in 2023 as EV Editor after six years at EVO India, where he rose to Deputy Editor. At EVO, he launched the magazine's EV vertical at a time when most Indian automotive publications were still treating electric vehicles as a footnote.

His testing methodology is unusually rigorous for Indian automotive journalism: every range claim is tested on a defined route (Bengaluru outer ring road, 40% highway, 60% city), temperature-normalised, and reported as a range band rather than a single number. He was the first Indian journalist to publish a winter-range penalty analysis for Indian EVs.

Before EVO India, Rohan spent two years as a technical correspondent for Autocar India and holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering from NIT Surathkal. He has contributed to the BIS EV Safety Standards Committee as a technical observer and spoken at ACMA EV Conclave 2024 on charging infrastructure gaps.

Outside of work, Rohan is a semi-competitive cyclist and has completed the Bangalore–Chennai endurance ride three times — which he credits for giving him a genuine understanding of range anxiety.
