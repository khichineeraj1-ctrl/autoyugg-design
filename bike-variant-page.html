Why we recommend it

The sweet spot
of the Hunter 350 lineup.

4.6/ 5

If you're cross-shopping the Hunter 350 lineup, the Metro Rebel is the variant we recommend to most buyers. You get the full 349cc engine — meaning the honest 380–420 km of real-world range — and the 70 kW fuel tank capacity that makes highway trips practical. The cabin tech is the same as the top variants: 12.3" infotainment, wireless BikePlay, panoramic sunroof, 9-speaker Tripper pod, 6 airbags.

What you skip vs Metro Dapper: ABS + Slipper clutch, Tripper Nav (Vehicle-to-Load), ventilated seats, and the Tripper navigation. If you drive mostly in city traffic where ABS isn't useful and don't camp/tailgate often, the ₹1.5 L savings is real money — closer to a free year of EMI than a feature compromise.

The 349 cc pack also means you avoid the 349 cc's biggest weakness — its 36 kmpl claimed mileage translates to 240–270 km in real driving, which gets tight outside city limits. Metro Rebel gives you EV range you'll never feel anxious about.
