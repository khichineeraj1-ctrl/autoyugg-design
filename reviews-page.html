First EV — and the right one to start with

Switched from a Creta diesel after 5 years. The Curvv EV has changed my daily commute completely. Mumbai-Pune-Mumbai easily in single charge. Charging at home overnight = ₹120 vs ₹3,500 of petrol weekly. ADAS in Mumbai traffic is a stress reliever. Only complaint: rear visibility is limited because of the coupé shape — 360 cam helps. Service experience at Tata has been smooth so far. Highly recommended for city + occasional highway use.

Great car, just plan around the boot

Family of 4 with regular outstation trips. Range and performance: 5/5. Cabin and tech: 5/5. Boot is the gripe — coupé shape eats real cargo space. A standard golf bag fits, but a stack of 3 large suitcases for a family vacation is a struggle. We bought a roof box. Otherwise no regrets — and the EMI is barely more than petrol bills used to be. Build quality is mostly good but I have noticed some rattle from the rear parcel shelf area at speed.

Good car held back by inconsistent service

The car itself is genuinely good — range is honest, charging works, ADAS is impressive. My problem has been after-sales. Two service appointments in 4 months, both required follow-up calls because parts were not in stock. Software update for the infotainment took 3 visits. If you live somewhere with a top-tier Tata service center, fine. In my area of Delhi, the EV-trained technicians are stretched thin and that shows. The car deserves 4.5; the experience around it pulls it down.
